How the radar values a protocol

implied = ecategory level × TVLslope × growth × maturity × breadth

  • Category level — the log-log fit log(market cap) = level + slope × log(TVL) on the tokened protocols in the same category, taken from this snapshot. The level is the category's median residual, shrunk toward the global level; categories with fewer than five tokened protocols use the global level. The table shows the multiple the level implies at the category's typical TVL — the multiple falls as TVL grows, because the fitted slope is below 1.
  • Growth — 1 + 0.5 × the 7-day TVL change, clamped to [−60%, +120%].
  • Maturity — 0.6 + 0.4 × min(days since listing, 1095) / 1095: a protocol that has waited years without a token is worth more than a new one.
  • Breadth — +5% for each extra chain, up to four.
  • Range — the same product with the category's 25th and 75th percentile levels instead of the median. A wide range means the market disagrees about the kind.
  • Validated out-of-sample — trained on tokens listed before a date and tested on later ones, the model lands within 2× of the real market cap 33% of the time (median error 3.6×), against 30% (5.3×) for the flat-multiple model it replaces. The full numbers are in the repository's README.

    How to read this board

    1. Rank by implied value, not by TVL. A big protocol of a kind the market prices cheaply is worth less than a smaller one of a kind the market prices richly.
    2. Mind the range. A narrow range means the category agrees on a price; a wide one means the implied value is a guess. Prefer narrow ranges when you pick one farm.
    3. Check the comparables. Every detail view names the tokened protocols the multiple came from. If you would not hold those tokens, do not farm their cousins.
    4. Farm only what the plan says. Each detail view carries eight steps with a cost and a time, sized from the protocol's own TVL and 7-day move.
    5. Implied value is a model estimate, not a price. No token exists yet, so nothing here is a promise of a launch, an airdrop, or a return.

    Values are model estimates from the sealed snapshot. They are not prices, not advice, and not a promise that any token will launch.
